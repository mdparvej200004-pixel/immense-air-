02

DLT Registration & Regulatory Compliance

  • All commercial SMS, promotional, transactional, and service campaigns in India require valid Principal Entity (PE) ID, Header (Sender ID), and approved Content Templates registered on an authorized telecom DLT portal.
  • Clients must maintain accurate PE-TM (Telemarketer) chain hashing as mandated by TRAI. Immense Air is not liable for operator rejections arising from unapproved headers, expired templates, or incomplete DLT chain bindings.
  • For Meta WhatsApp Business API and RCS Messaging, clients must comply with Meta Commerce & Business Messaging policies and Google Jibe/carrier verification guidelines respectively.
